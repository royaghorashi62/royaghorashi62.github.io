Research

My dissertation was about drawing fair political districts. Below it are two other Ph.D. projects and my personal side projects.

Ph.D. dissertation · UWM

Math Models and Heuristic Methods for Constructing Fair Political Districts

I built exact math models and a heuristic model that draw equitable political districts, then applied them to Wisconsin's real political, demographic, and geographic data to redraw the State of Wisconsin political map.

  • Developed math models (MIP and column generation) and proposed Simulated Annealing as a heuristic for constructing fair political districts.
  • Coded the exact models in CPLEX and the heuristic in C++.
  • Collected and cleaned Wisconsin political, demographic, and geographic data with ArcGIS and R.
  • Applied the algorithm to form Wisconsin State Assembly, State Senate and U.S. Congressional districts.
